What is Agentic AI?
Agentic AI autonomously perceives social media streams, maintains context across interactions, reasons over complex data (sentiment, trends, competitors), and takes goal-directed actions — generating campaigns, forecasting trends, scheduling posts — without needing step-by-step human instruction for every task.
